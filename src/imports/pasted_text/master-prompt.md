Build a complete, production-ready personal portfolio website for **Hammad Ayodi**, a digital marketing expert, growth strategist, freelancer, and solution architect.

The website must feel **luxurious, editorial, intelligent, human, sophisticated, calm, credible and highly intentional**.

It must NOT look like an AI-generated portfolio.

It must NOT look like a generic SaaS landing page.

It must NOT use the typical AI aesthetic:

* excessive gradients
* purple/blue neon
* glassmorphism
* floating glowing blobs
* excessive rounded cards
* generic dashboard UI
* meaningless statistics
* overuse of icons
* cartoon illustrations
* excessive animations
* template-looking sections
* "AI-generated" visual language

The design direction should take inspiration from the **editorial luxury and authentic visual restraint of Maison Camion**, while being completely original and tailored to Hammad's personal brand.

REFERENCE:
https://www.maisoncamion.com/

Do not copy Maison Camion's design, content, branding, assets, layouts or exact visual identity.

Instead, understand the principles:

* luxury through restraint
* strong typography
* editorial composition
* generous whitespace

* sophisticated photography
* subtle motion
* premium art direction
* excellent typography hierarchy
* confident minimalism
* human craftsmanship
